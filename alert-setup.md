# Job alert setup (about 15 minutes, once)

The nightly run reads job alert emails in the Gmail inbox Claude reads. If your CV uses a different address, point every alert at
the Gmail inbox Claude reads, or forward alerts from the other inbox to it.

## LinkedIn (laptop, Chrome)
1. Go to linkedin.com/jobs and search each keyword below with location "Dhaka, Bangladesh".
2. Turn on "Set alert" / "Job alert" for each search, frequency Daily, by email.
3. Settings > Communications > Email: make sure job alert emails are on and go to the Gmail inbox Claude reads.

Keywords (one alert each): CRM executive, customer engagement, customer success, customer experience,
digital marketing executive, marketing executive, key account executive, business development executive.

## BDjobs
1. Log in at bdjobs.com, open My Bdjobs > Job Alert (or "Create Job Alert").
2. Category: Marketing/Sales, and Customer Service/Call Centre. Location: Dhaka.
3. Keywords: CRM, customer engagement, digital marketing, marketing executive, customer success, retention.
4. Delivery: daily email to the Gmail inbox Claude reads.

## Skill.jobs
1. Sign up or log in at skill.jobs, set job alerts for the same keywords, location Dhaka, daily email.
